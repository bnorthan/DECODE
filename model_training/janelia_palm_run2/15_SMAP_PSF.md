## SMAP PSF fit

Once we have extracted some beads start the SMAP GUI from MATLAB and run SMAP PSF fit by choosing ```Analyze->sr3D->calibrate3DsplinePSF```

![alt text](img_menu_calibrate3dsplinePSF.png)

## SMAP Decode training estimates 

We can also use SMAP to estimate the Decode training parameters.  Load a series of frames and choose ```Analyze->calibrate->DECODE_training_estimates```

![alt text](img_menu_deconde_training_estimages.png)
